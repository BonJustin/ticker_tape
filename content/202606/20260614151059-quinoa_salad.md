keywords: [recipe, salad]
---

### Salad

| ½ cup dried quinoa, cooked and cooled
| ½ English cucumber, diced
| ½ red bell pepper, diced
| 1-2 large handfuls of greens
|   *spinach, bok choy, arugula, microgreens*
| More veggies
|   *grated carrot, broccoli stems*
| Chopped parsley, cilantro, basil, mint, or dill
| Seeds or nuts
|   *sunflower seeds, walnuts, pine nuts, or pepitas*
| Dried fruit
|   *blueberries, cranberries…*

---

### Dressing

| 1:1 ratio olive oil to apple cider vinegar
| Dijon mustard
| Honey or maple syrup
| Salt to taste
| Black pepper
| Lemon or lime juice

---

* Cook the quinoa. Rinse, then use a 2:1 ratio water to quinoa. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook 10 minutes. Let rest for 5 minutes, then chill in the fridge.
* Toss it all together.
