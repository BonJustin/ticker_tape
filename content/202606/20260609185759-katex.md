### aligned {#aligned}

*Align equations… each line of the equation will align on `&`, and new lines are created with `\\`.*

```
\begin{aligned}
a - (b + c) &= a + (-1)(b + c)\\
&= a + (-1)b + (-1)c\\
&= a - b - c
\end{aligned}
```

$$
\begin{aligned}
a - (b + c) &= a + (-1)(b + c)\\
&= a + (-1)b + (-1)c\\
&= a - b - c
\end{aligned}
$$

### radicals {#radicals}

```
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{ab} &= \sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} \\
\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} &= \frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} \\
a^{\frac{x}{y}} &= \left(\sqrt[y]{a}\right)^x = \sqrt[y]{a^x}
\end{aligned}
```

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{ab} &= \sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} \\
\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} &= \frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} \\
a^{\frac{x}{y}} &= \left(\sqrt[y]{a}\right)^x = \sqrt[y]{a^x}
\end{aligned}
$$
