*From [The Full Helping](https://www.thefullhelping.com/purpose-cashew-cream-recipe/)*

Cashew cream is simply blended cashews and water. Soak cashews overnight in the fridge with cold water, for 4 hours at room temperature with cold water, or at least 15-30 minutes with boiling water to soften, then rinse under cold water prior to blending.

### Ingredients

| 1 cup cashews
| ½ cup water
| ¼ tsp salt

### Savory additions

| 2 teaspoons lemon juice
| 1 clove minced garlic
| paprika
| onion powder

### Sweet additions

| 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
| 1-2 tablespoons maple syrup
| ¼ teaspoon cinammon
|   *cardamom, ginger, chai spice*
