| 1 slice of bacon
| ½ medium onion, chopped
| 1 rib celery, chopped
| 1 clove garlic, minced
| ½ teaspoon salt
| ¼ teaspoon dried thyme. (Or fresh.)
| 2 cups broth
| 2 medium potatoes
| 1½ cups fresh corn
| ⅓ - ½ cup whole milk
| 1 tablespoons cornstarch
| cheese and green onions for garnish

### Instructions

1. In a large Dutch oven over medium heat, brown the bacon.
2. Add onion and celery, stir until the onion starts to brown. Add garlic, salt, thyme, and pepper briefly.
3. Stir in the broth and deglaze the pot.
4. Add potatoes and corn. Bring to a simmer and lower the temperature.
5. Simmer until the potatoes are tender.
6. Separately whisk the milk and cornstarch and ***slowly*** stir into the simmering soup a little at a time, until thickened.
7. Turn off the heat. Check the flavor and adjust if needed. Garnish with cheese, green onion, or more bacon, or whatever.
