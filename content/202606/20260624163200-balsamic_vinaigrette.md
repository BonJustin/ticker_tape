---
title: Balsamic Vinaigrette
keywords: [recipe, dressing, salad]
---

*Makes around ½ cup*

***

| 1 tablespoon shallot
| 1 tablespoon balsamic vinaigrette
| 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
| 4 tablespoons olive oil
| salt
| pepper

***

Macerate the shallot in the red wine vinegar before adding the other ingredients.
