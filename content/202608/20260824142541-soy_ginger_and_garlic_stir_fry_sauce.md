---
title: Soy, Ginger, & Garlic Sauce
keywords: [recipe, sauce]
---

*This works well as a stir-fry sauce.*

---

| ¼ cup soy sauce
| 1 tbs honey or sugar
| 1 tbs mirin or rice wine
| 1 tbs rice vinegar
| 1 tbs water
| 2 tsp cornstarch
| 1 tsp sesame oil
| 1 tbs minced shallot
| 1-2 cloves minced garlic
| 2 tsp minced ginger
| olive oil

---

Saute the garlic, ginger, and shallot with the base of the stir fry. Once the stir-fry is mostly cooked, add the liquid and heat until it thickens. I tried it with broccoli and bell pepper served over rice, and it was delicious.
