---
title: Pita Bread
keywords: [recipe, bread]
---

| 1 cup lukewarm water
| 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
| ½ teaspoon sugar
| 3 cups flour
| 1 to 2 teaspoons salt
| 2 tablespoons olive oil

___

1. **Sponge:** Mix water, yeast, and sugar, and ½ cup flour. Let it stand for 15 minutes until it bubbles.
2. **Form the dough:** Add salt, olive oil, and most of the flour, and stir.
3. **Knead:** Just a bit until smooth, then let it stand for 10 minutes and knead again. Dust with flour as needed.
4. **Let rise:** Coat the dough with olive oil, cover, and let rise until doubled.
5. **Divide:** Make around 8 balls of dough, let them rest again. These can be refrigerated for a few days.
6. **Bake:** Heat the oven to 475° F And bake the first side for 2 minutes, and the second side for 1. Or cook on a very hot skillet on the stovetop.
