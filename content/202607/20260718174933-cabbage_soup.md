---
title: Cabbage Soup
keywords: [recipe, soup]
---

| olive oil
| celery, onion, and carrot mirepoix
| thyme
| chili flakes
| garlic
| cannelinni beans
| salt
| pepper
| broth
| canned or fresh tomatoes
| cabbage
|   *I tried this with fire roasted canned and it was fine.*
| parsley

---

* Saute the mirepoix until the onions begin to go translucent. Add spices and rinsed beans until the garlic is aromatic. Then add broth and tomatoes and bring to a simmer. Add the cabbage. Cook until the cabbage tastes right, add the parsley briefly before removing from the heat.
