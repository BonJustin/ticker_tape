### Teriyaki

| ½ cup tamari
| ¼ cup brown sugar
| 1½ teaspoon minced fresh ginger
| 1 teaspoon minced garlic
| 1 tablespoon honey
| 1 teaspoon sesame oil
| 3 tablespoons mirin
| 3 teaspoons cornstarch in ¼ cup water

---

* Heat gently until thickened.


### Dipping sauce

| 1 cup pineapple juice
| ¾ cup brown sugar
| ⅓ cup rice vinegar
| 3 tablespoons ketchup
| 2 tablespoons tamari
| 1½ tablespoons cornstarch in 2 tablespoons water

---

* Heat gently until thickened.
