---
title: "Spagetti Squash"
keywords: [recipe]
---

* Preheat the oven to 425°.
* Chop the spagetti squash in half lengthwise.
* Scrape out the seedy middle with a spoon. (Save the seeds for toasting.)
* Drizzle evenly or rub with olive oil.
* Add salt, pepper, garlic, and parsley.
* Place the halves cut side down on a baking sheet with parchment paper.
* Bake until the skin is easily pierced and the insides feel soft.
