Enough brine for 4-6 eggs?

This requires making some gently boiled eggs for **6.5** to **8** minutes, depending on volume and preferences. I usually do 4 eggs for 7.25 minutes. You can add baking soda to the water to make them slightly easier to peel, *but I don't*. Immediately put the eggs in an ice water bath until cool, then peel, and best of luck with that! (The age of the egg changes the way it peels due to additional air diffusing into the egg.)

***

For **white** seasoned eggs:

| 1 cup water
| ¼ cup sake
| 1 tbs sugar
| 1 tsp salt

***

For **brown** seasoned eggs:

| 4 tbs soy sauce
| 4 tbs mirin
| water to adjust the saltiness (6-12 tbs?)

***

Submerge the peeled eggs in the brine. I use a mason jar. Place in the fridge overnight before eating.
