| **Beans**
| 1 can plain fava beans
|     *rinsed and drained*
| ¼ cup water
| salt
| ¼ to ½ tsp ground cumin
| 
| **Lemon-Garlic Sauce**
| 1 or 2 jalapeños
|     *roughly chopped, save some for garnish*
| 1 clove garlic
|     *course chop*
| lemon juice
|     *around ½ lemon's worth?*
| 
| **For Serving**
| olive oil
| ½ cup parsley
|     *coursely chopped*
| 1 tomato
|     *diced*
| pita, sliced veggies, and olives

***

Cook the beans in a heavy skillet with water over medium-high heat. Stir until heated, 5 to 7 minutes. Season the beans with salt and cumin. Mash some until chunky and stew-like. Mash the peppers and garlic into a course paste, and add to the lemon juice. Pour the sauce over the warm beans in the pan, add a drizzle of olive oil, top with tomato and parsly and reseved sliced peppers. Serve with pita, sliced vegetables, and olives on the side.
