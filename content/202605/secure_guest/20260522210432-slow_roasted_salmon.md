---
title: Slow-Roasted Salmon
keywords: [recipe, salmon]
---

| lemon
| olive oil
| salt
| parsley

Preheat oven to 225° or as low as your oven can manage.

Put parchment paper on a baking sheet. Place a layer of thinly sliced lemon and some chopped parsley. Season the salmon fillet with salt and place on the lemons. Spread lemon zest and olive oil on the fillet. Slow-roast for around 30-40 minutes. Check and turn occasionally, and brush with olive oil if dry. I don't mind crushing garlic, mixing the oil and lemon juice, and using that for a baste, similar to a lemon vinaigrette.

I've tried baking at different temperatures, covering, cast iron, foil, glazes, different herbs, spicing the fillet, etc. Honestly, they were all good. As long as the fat on the edges of the fillet turn nice and white and bubble, and a gentle touch can separate the middle on the seams, salmon is forgiving at low temperatures. I like it best with some rice, lemon juice, and generous parsley and shallot herb salsa.
