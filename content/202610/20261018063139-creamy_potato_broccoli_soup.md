***

*This made just enough to fill my 2 quart dutch oven.*

½ yellow onion \
1 large carrot \
2-3 celery stalks \
1 head brocolli florets \
1 large yellow potato \
½ cup corn \
3-4 cups broth \
2-3 cloves garlic \
1 tsp oregano \
1 tsp thyme \
½ tsp sage \
2 tbs butter \
⅙ cup flour \
¼ cup heavy cream \
½ tsp balsamic vinegar

***

1. Melt the butter and saute the onion, carrot, and celery mirepoix with a pinch of salt and pepper over medium-high heat.
2. Lower the heat and add minced garlic, oregano, thyme, and sage and cook briefly.
3. Add the flour and stir to coat. Let the flour brown on the bottom of the pot.
4. Add a generous splash of broth and gently deglaze the pot.
5. Add the remaining broth and diced potaoes and corn. Adjust salt and pepper, and add chili flakes if desired.
6. Cover and bring to a simmer, add the broccoli, then lower the heat and cook until the potatoes are the desired texture.
7. Remove from the heat and slowly stir in the heavy cream and vinegar.
8. If desired add cheese. *I generally do not.*
9. Garnish with parsley, cheddar, crackers, and possibly a slight drizzle of oil.
