Farenheit to centrigrade conversion

\begin{aligned} \text{freezing point of water} &= 32^{\circ}\mathrm{F} = 0^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\\ \text{boiling point of water} &= 212^{\circ}\mathrm{F} = 100^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\\ ^{\circ}\mathrm{F} &= \frac{9}{5}^{\circ}\mathrm{C} + 32\\ ^{\circ}\mathrm{F} - 32 &= \frac{9}{5}^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\\ \frac{5}{9} \left( ^{\circ}\mathrm{F} - 32 \right) &= ^{\circ}\mathrm{C} \end{aligned}
